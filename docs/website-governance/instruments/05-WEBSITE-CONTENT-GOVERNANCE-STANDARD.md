# Website Content Governance Standard

**Document ID:** CLN-WEB-STD-005  
**Version:** 1.2.0  
**Status:** Ready for Founder Ratification  
**Authority:** CLN-WEB-CON-001  
**Owner:** Website Business Owner  
**Canonical Rule Ownership:** Content lifecycle, public claims, evidence, AI-assisted content, correction, and retirement  
**Effective Date:** Upon ratification  
**Review Cycle:** Annual; core business and form content at least semiannually  
**Supersedes:** CLN-WEB-STD-005 v1.1.0  

---

## 1. Purpose

This Standard governs how website content is drafted, approved, published, reviewed, corrected, and retired.

It is the single normative home for the website’s public claims and evidence rule.

---

## 2. Content Principles

Published content must be:

- Accurate.
- Useful.
- Owned.
- Approved.
- Supportable.
- Current enough for its purpose.
- Placed within the approved information architecture.
- Written for a commercial audience.

Content shall not be published solely because it is available, generated quickly, or expected to increase traffic.

---

## 3. Content Classes

- Core business content.
- Service content.
- Industry content.
- Evidence content.
- Resource content.
- Opportunity content.
- Legal and data content.
- Technical and routing content.

The applicable page structure is governed by CLN-WEB-STD-003.

---

## 4. Proportional Content Record

### 4.1 Routine Content Record

A routine substantive page needs:

1. Title and URL.
2. Purpose and audience.
3. Owner.
4. Primary action.
5. Approval.
6. Last and next review date.
7. Claims requiring evidence, or “none.”

### 4.2 Expanded Record

Add only the fields relevant to risk:

- Evidence location.
- Permission record.
- Privacy or form impact.
- Integration impact.
- Legal review.
- Accessibility review.
- Superseded source.
- Rollback or correction plan.

A spelling correction or non-substantive formatting change requires only a lightweight change-log entry.

---

## 5. Lifecycle

`Proposed → Draft → Review → Approved → Published → Revision Required → Retired`

Archive only when preservation is required for evidence, legal, rollback, or governance purposes.

A draft is not authority. Publication does not cure missing approval.

---

## 6. Claims and Evidence

A material public claim must be:

- True.
- Current enough for its use.
- Properly scoped.
- Supported by retained evidence.
- Approved for disclosure.
- Removed, qualified, or revised when support expires.

Material claims include:

- Numerical outcomes.
- Quality or completion rates.
- Years in business.
- Customer or facility counts.
- Geographic coverage.
- Insurance, bonding, certification, and license statements.
- Regulatory or safety claims.
- Named client relationships.
- Testimonials and client logos.
- Comparative or superlative claims.
- Guarantees.

AI output, memory, common industry language, and competitor copy are not evidence.

---

## 7. Writing Requirements

Content shall:

- Use clear commercial language.
- Explain the visitor’s problem and the relevant service.
- Distinguish service categories.
- State exclusions or qualifications where needed.
- Explain the next step.
- Avoid internal software vocabulary unless the visitor needs it.
- Comply with CLN-WEB-STD-006.
- Comply with CLN-WEB-POL-004 for geography.
- Comply with CLN-WEB-POL-007 for calls to action.

---

## 8. Service and Industry Duplication

Service and industry content shall follow the distinct-value rule in CLN-WEB-STD-003 §6.

Where two pages compete for the same purpose or substantially duplicate sections, improve one canonical page rather than publishing both.

---

## 9. Confidentiality and Permission

Content shall not disclose:

- Private pricing or contract terms.
- Facility vulnerabilities.
- Access or security details.
- Private contact information.
- Unapproved worker identities.
- Operational incidents.
- Private communications.
- Tenant or internal identifiers.

Client identification, logos, testimonials, photographs, and case studies require approval or anonymization.

---

## 10. Images and Media

Media must:

- Be owned, licensed, or approved.
- Accurately represent the work or be clearly illustrative.
- Avoid false before-and-after manipulation.
- Avoid confidential facility details.
- Include appropriate alternative text.
- Be optimized for performance.
- Be removed when rights or accuracy expire.

---

## 11. AI-Assisted Content

AI may assist drafting and analysis.

Before publication, an accountable human reviewer must verify:

- Business and service accuracy.
- Claims and evidence.
- Geography.
- Confidentiality and permissions.
- Legal or compliance wording.
- Links and calls to action.
- Tone.
- Duplication.
- Accessibility.
- Absence of fabricated facts.

---

## 12. Review Triggers

Review is required when:

- A service changes.
- Service territory changes.
- A form or portal changes.
- A claim is challenged or expires.
- A client withdraws permission.
- A legal, privacy, or security requirement changes.
- An incident affects public content.
- Content begins creating materially unqualified inquiries.

---

## 13. Correction

Inaccurate or misleading content shall be removed, qualified, or corrected.

A correction is material when it changes a public promise, claim, service scope, territory meaning, legal notice, data practice, or user decision.

Material corrections use CLN-WEB-REG-009. Minor corrections use the lightweight log.

---

## 14. Prohibitions

Do not publish:

- Fabricated testimonials, clients, results, or urgency.
- Unsupported guarantees.
- Plagiarized or competitor-derived copy.
- Unreviewed AI output.
- Confidential information.
- Keyword-stuffed or auto-proliferated pages.
- Claims that cleaning guarantees client compliance.
- Content inconsistent with current capability.
- City-level service-area content prohibited by CLN-WEB-POL-004.

---

## 15. Evidence

Maintain, as applicable:

- Page approvals.
- Evidence files.
- Permission records.
- Review dates.
- Corrections.
- Retirement and redirect decisions.
- Current form and legal copy.
