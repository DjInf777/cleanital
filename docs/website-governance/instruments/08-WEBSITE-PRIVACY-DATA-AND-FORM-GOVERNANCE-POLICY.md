# Website Privacy, Data, and Form Governance Policy

**Document ID:** CLN-WEB-POL-008  
**Version:** 1.2.0  
**Status:** Ready for Founder Ratification  
**Authority:** CLN-WEB-CON-001; CLN-WEB-POL-002  
**Owner:** Website Business Owner  
**Required Reviewers:** Technical Owner; Privacy and Security Reviewer  
**Incident Owner:** Founder unless another Website Incident Owner is recorded  
**Canonical Rule Ownership:** Public website data, forms, tracking, processors, retention, and data incidents  
**Effective Date:** Upon ratification  
**Review Cycle:** Annual and before a material data, tracking, vendor, or communication change  
**Supersedes:** CLN-WEB-POL-008 v1.1.0  

---

## 1. Purpose and Limitation

This Policy establishes Cleanital’s internal website privacy and data controls.

It is not legal advice and does not by itself establish legal compliance.

Before public launch, a qualified legal reviewer shall review, as applicable:

- The privacy notice.
- Cookie and tracking disclosures.
- Assessment and contact consent language.
- Employment and contractor collection.
- Marketing email practices.
- Telephone and text practices.
- Data-retention language.
- Accessibility statement language.
- Applicable federal and state requirements.

---

## 2. Legal Reference Triggers

The legal review shall expressly consider, where applicable:

- The CAN-SPAM Act and Federal Trade Commission rules and guidance for commercial email.
- The Telephone Consumer Protection Act and Federal Communications Commission rules for certain automated, prerecorded, or marketing calls and texts.
- Applicable state privacy, data-security, breach-notification, employment, and contract requirements.
- Children’s privacy requirements if the website is ever directed to children or knowingly collects children’s information.

A website inquiry does not automatically provide consent for every future communication method.

---

## 3. Privacy Principles

Website data shall follow:

- Purpose limitation.
- Data minimization.
- Accuracy.
- Transparency.
- Limited access.
- Secure transmission and storage.
- Controlled retention.
- Safe deletion.
- Vendor accountability.
- No undisclosed secondary use.

---

## 4. Data Categories

- Public content.
- Business contact data.
- Prospective-client data.
- Employment-interest data.
- Contractor-interest data.
- Analytics data.
- Technical and security logs.
- Approved uploads.
- Minimal portal-routing data.

---

## 5. Collection Approval

A new field, tracker, cookie, script, upload, integration, or data category requires:

1. Purpose and owner.
2. Data collected.
3. Required or optional status.
4. Notice or consent.
5. Destination and access.
6. Retention and deletion.
7. Security and failure behavior.

External processor, legal review, and incident impacts are added when relevant.

---

## 6. Prohibited Public Collection

Unless separately authorized with documented safeguards, do not collect:

- Passwords.
- Banking or payment-card credentials.
- Government identification numbers.
- Health or biometric data.
- Access or alarm codes.
- Detailed security procedures.
- Background-check data.
- Children’s personal information.
- Unnecessary identity documents.
- Confidential operational records.

Free-text forms should warn users not to submit sensitive information.

---

## 7. Use and Access

Website data may be used only for the disclosed and approved purpose.

Access shall be limited by business need.

Employment and contractor information should be separated from general commercial access where practical.

Local exports and spreadsheets remain governed copies and shall be deleted when no longer needed.

---

## 8. Transmission and Storage

Website data shall:

- Use encrypted transport.
- Go only to approved destinations.
- Avoid sensitive content in URLs.
- Avoid secrets in client-side code.
- Avoid public indexing.
- Use approved storage.
- Be removed from temporary locations when no longer needed.

Direct unrestricted public access to Corp4ce databases is prohibited.

---

## 9. Attachments

Public upload is disabled by default.

An approved upload must define:

- Purpose.
- File types and size.
- Malware protection.
- Storage.
- Access.
- Retention and deletion.
- User warning.
- Failure behavior.

Uploads shall not be public by default.

---

## 10. Analytics and Cookies

Analytics may measure content usefulness, assessment conversion, routing, usability, acquisition, and technical performance.

Analytics shall not collect form content, credentials, or confidential information unless specifically approved and legally reviewed.

A tracker or cookie requires an inventory entry covering purpose, data, retention, sharing, user control, and removal.

The public notice must describe the technologies actually in use.

---

## 11. Third-Party Processors

A processor must have:

- Approved purpose.
- Limited data scope.
- Reasonable security.
- Appropriate contractual terms.
- Retention or deletion behavior.
- Controlled access.
- Exit capability.

Ease of installation is not a sufficient reason to add a vendor.

---

## 12. Retention

Each data category requires a retention rule.

- Spam and unqualified submissions should be removed when no longer needed.
- Qualified opportunities should move into the approved system of record.
- Website copies should not remain indefinitely after migration.
- Employment and contractor data follows the applicable governed process.
- Security logs and analytics shall be retained only as long as justified.
- Legal holds and binding obligations override ordinary deletion.

---

## 13. Requests

Provide a public method for privacy-related requests.

Requests shall be appropriately verified, routed, evaluated, completed or answered, and recorded when material.

---

## 14. Website Incident Ownership

The Website Incident Owner has authority to declare a website data or security incident.

The response shall include, as applicable:

1. Containment.
2. Evidence preservation.
3. Scope and data assessment.
4. Credential or key rotation.
5. Vendor coordination.
6. Legal and notification review.
7. Correction.
8. Recovery validation.
9. Post-incident record.

A material incident must be recorded in CLN-WEB-REG-009.

---

## 15. Marketing Communication Controls

A request submission authorizes communication about that request using reasonable methods disclosed to the user.

Before Cleanital sends unrelated promotional email, automated text, autodialed call, or prerecorded message, the responsible owner shall verify applicable consent, identification, opt-out, recordkeeping, and suppression requirements through qualified legal review.

Marketing preference data shall not be inferred from operational consent.

---

## 16. Prohibitions

Do not:

- Sell identifiable submission data.
- Publish submissions.
- Use data for undisclosed unrelated purposes.
- Place credentials or sensitive content in analytics.
- Send sensitive fields through URLs.
- Retain data indefinitely without justification.
- Give vendors unrestricted access.
- Use production data for testing without protection.
- Publish a privacy or accessibility claim that is not implemented.
- Display false success when delivery failed.

---

## 17. Evidence

Maintain:

- Form and field inventory.
- Tracker and cookie inventory.
- Processor inventory.
- Data-flow summary.
- Access assignments.
- Retention rules.
- Incident records.
- Legal-review dispositions.
- Current public privacy notice.
