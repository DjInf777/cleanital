# Brand Voice and Messaging Standard

**Document ID:** CLN-WEB-STD-006  
**Version:** 1.2.0  
**Status:** Ready for Founder Ratification  
**Authority:** CLN-WEB-CON-001  
**Owner:** Website Business Owner  
**Canonical Rule Ownership:** Public voice, positioning, terminology, and messaging structure  
**Effective Date:** Upon ratification  
**Review Cycle:** Annual and upon material positioning change  
**Supersedes:** CLN-WEB-STD-006 v1.1.0  

---

## 1. Positioning

Cleanital is a commercial janitorial and facility-cleaning company.

The website shall communicate dependable service, clear expectations, professional communication, quality practices, and assessment before commitment.

It shall not position Cleanital as a residential cleaning service.

---

## 2. Mandatory Commercial-Scope Statement

The website shall clearly state, on the Home page and the Request an Assessment page, that Cleanital focuses on commercial facilities and organizations.

Equivalent wording is permitted.

This requirement is intended to reduce residential and consumer inquiries without excluding legitimate small businesses that use personal-domain email addresses.

---

## 3. Voice

The approved voice is:

- Professional.
- Direct.
- Operationally competent.
- Educational.
- Confident.
- Responsible.
- Commercially oriented.

Avoid a voice that is exaggerated, desperate, residential, fear-based, legally conclusive, or mechanically generated.

---

## 4. Messaging Order

Public messaging should answer:

1. What Cleanital does.
2. Who Cleanital serves.
3. Where the public territory is defined.
4. What facility problem is addressed.
5. How Cleanital approaches the work.
6. What evidence supports credibility.
7. What the visitor should do next.

Geographic language is governed only by CLN-WEB-POL-004.

---

## 5. Approved Brand Concept

Approved concept:

> Cleanital provides dependable commercial janitorial and facility-cleaning services for organizations in North Carolina and South Carolina.

A page using this short statement must satisfy the contextual qualification rule in CLN-WEB-POL-004 §3. The statement itself does not guarantee universal serviceability.

---

## 6. Preferred Terminology

Preferred terms:

- Client.
- Prospective client.
- Organization.
- Facility.
- Account.
- Location.
- Commercial cleaning.
- Recurring janitorial service.
- Day porter service.
- Floor care.
- Project cleaning.
- Post-construction cleaning.
- Service assessment.
- Scope of work.
- Quality verification.
- Service availability.
- Secure portal.

“Client” is preferred to “customer” when describing an ongoing business relationship.

---

## 7. Service Distinctions

Messaging must preserve these distinctions:

- Recurring janitorial service is scheduled ongoing cleaning.
- Day porter service supports designated daytime or operating-hour needs.
- Office cleaning concerns professional workplaces.
- Retail cleaning concerns customer-facing retail environments.
- Strip and wax is a restorative floor-care process.
- Scrub and recoat refreshes an existing finish where conditions allow.
- Commercial floor care is the parent service category.
- Post-construction cleaning addresses construction dust, debris, residues, and readiness.
- Project cleaning is a defined non-recurring scope.

---

## 8. Compliance Language

Cleanital may explain that its work supports sanitation, presentation, operational readiness, and facility-care requirements.

It shall not claim that cleaning:

- Guarantees legal or regulatory compliance.
- Guarantees an inspection result.
- Eliminates all risk.
- Transfers the client’s legal responsibility.
- Certifies a facility.

---

## 9. Calls to Action

The primary commercial call to action is governed by CLN-WEB-POL-007.

Approved secondary actions may include:

- Explore Services.
- View Industries.
- Learn How We Work.
- Contact Cleanital.
- Visit the Client Portal.
- Visit the Worker Portal.
- Explore Careers.
- Explore Contractor Opportunities.

Do not use “Book Instantly,” “Confirm Service,” or “Guaranteed Pricing” unless the governing model is formally changed.

---

## 10. Forms and Status Messages

Forms shall:

- Explain their purpose.
- Distinguish required and optional fields.
- Avoid implying acceptance.
- Use truthful success and failure messages.

Errors shall explain the next safe step without exposing technical or private details.

---

## 11. Language to Avoid

Avoid unsupported or misleading phrases such as:

- Best cleaning company.
- Available everywhere.
- Perfect every time.
- Guaranteed compliance.
- Lowest price guaranteed.
- Instant quote.
- No job too big or small.
- 100% guaranteed.

A precise, approved guarantee may be used only when scope, remedy, duration, exclusions, and evidence are documented.

---

## 12. Approval Test

Before publication, verify:

- Is it true?
- Is the commercial audience clear?
- Is the service accurately described?
- Is geography compliant by cross-reference to CLN-WEB-POL-004?
- Is the next step clear?
- Are claims supported?
- Does wording avoid unintended commitments?
- Does it preserve the website/Corp4ce boundary?
