# Website Decision and Change Register

**Document ID:** CLN-WEB-REG-009  
**Version:** 1.2.0  
**Status:** Active upon Phase 1.1 ratification  
**Authority:** CLN-WEB-CON-001  
**Owner:** Cleanital Founder  
**Canonical Rule Ownership:** Material decisions, amendments, exceptions, supersessions, and material nonconformance records  
**Effective Date:** Upon ratification  
**Review Cycle:** Continuous; quarterly during active development  
**Supersedes:** CLN-WEB-REG-009 v1.1.0  

---

## 1. Purpose

This Register records material website decisions without forcing routine editorial work through a full governance process.

Materiality and proportionality are defined in CLN-WEB-CTL-010.

Normative rules remain in their owning documents. Register entries record adoption, interpretation, amendment, exception, or supersession; they do not silently rewrite the owning instrument.

---

## 2. Decision States

- Proposed.
- Approved.
- Effective.
- Rejected.
- Superseded.
- Expired.
- Withdrawn.

Only Approved and Effective decisions authorize work.

---

## 3. Proportional Record Types

### 3.1 Full Decision Record

Required for constitutional, boundary, territory, data-category, legal commitment, system-of-record, primary-conversion, or other High-impact changes.

Required fields:

1. Decision ID and title.
2. Status and decision type.
3. Governing rule.
4. Exact current and new rule.
5. Rationale.
6. Affected artifacts and systems.
7. Relevant impact analysis.
8. Required reviewers and dispositions.
9. Implementation and validation.
10. Effective date.
11. Rollback or repeal method.
12. Founder disposition.

Only relevant impact categories must be completed. “Not applicable” is acceptable when reasoned.

### 3.2 Standard Change Record

Used for Medium-impact changes.

Fields:

- Change ID.
- Date.
- Reason.
- Affected artifact.
- Old and new meaning.
- Approver.
- Validation.
- Status.

### 3.3 Lightweight Change Log

Used for Low-impact changes.

Fields:

- Date.
- File or page.
- Concise change.
- Editor.
- Verification.

---

## 4. Constitutional Safeguard

A Register entry inconsistent with CLN-WEB-CON-001 is void unless the Constitution is first amended under its §16.

A Founder instruction, code change, page publication, file date, or newer Register entry does not bypass constitutional amendment.

## 6. Initial Ratification Mechanics

Founder approval of the Phase 1.1 package activates:

- This Register.
- The initial decisions in §5.
- The nine other instruments in the same ratified package.

The durable approval reference may be:

- A signed ratification page.
- A repository pull-request approval.
- A signed electronic record.
- A Founder decision record that identifies the package checksum or version.

This bootstrapping approval is valid even though the Register was not active before the approval event.

## 11. Supersession

A supersession must identify the old decision or instrument, exact old and new rule, implementation, effective date, and preserved evidence location.

Silence, publication, or file recency does not supersede authority.
