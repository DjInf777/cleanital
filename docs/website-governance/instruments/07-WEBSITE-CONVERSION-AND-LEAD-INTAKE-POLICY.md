# Website Conversion and Lead Intake Policy

**Document ID:** CLN-WEB-POL-007  
**Version:** 1.2.0  
**Status:** Ready for Founder Ratification  
**Authority:** CLN-WEB-CON-001; CLN-WEB-POL-002  
**Owner:** Website Business Owner  
**Canonical Rule Ownership:** Primary conversion, public intake paths, required fields, submission meaning, and conversion measures  
**Effective Date:** Upon ratification  
**Review Cycle:** Semiannual and before a material form or routing change  
**Supersedes:** CLN-WEB-POL-007 v1.1.0  

---

## 1. Primary Commercial Conversion

The primary commercial conversion is:

> Request an Assessment

An assessment request begins qualification. It does not:

- Create a client or contract.
- Confirm capacity.
- Confirm a service date.
- Bind pricing.
- Authorize work.
- Create portal access automatically.

---

## 2. Approved Paths

The website may provide:

1. Request an Assessment.
2. General Contact.
3. Career Interest.
4. Contractor Interest.
5. Active Client Portal.
6. Active Worker Portal.
7. Active Applicant Portal.
8. Active Contractor Portal.

Portal routes must satisfy CLN-WEB-POL-002.

---

## 3. Commercial-Scope Disclosure

The Home page and assessment page shall state that Cleanital focuses on commercial facilities and organizations.

Residential requests may be respectfully declined or routed elsewhere at Cleanital’s discretion. The website shall not imply that residential service is offered.

---

## 4. Assessment Purpose

The assessment form shall collect enough information to determine:

- Commercial fit.
- Public-territory fit under CLN-WEB-POL-004.
- Likely service need.
- Appropriate follow-up.
- Whether the opportunity warrants further qualification.

The form shall not replace discovery, site assessment, pricing, proposal, or contracting.

---

## 5. Baseline Fields

### Required

- Contact name.
- Organization name.
- Email address.
- Phone number.
- State.
- Facility or organization type.
- Service needed.
- Brief description.
- Consent to be contacted about the request.

A company-domain email is not required. Legitimate small businesses may use Gmail or another personal-domain provider.

### Optional or Conditional

- Facility address or postal code.
- Number of locations.
- Approximate size.
- Desired frequency.
- Desired start period.
- Current service situation.
- Preferred contact method.
- Best contact time.
- Approved file upload.

---

## 6. Prohibited Fields

Do not request through general public forms:

- Passwords or portal credentials.
- Banking or payment-card data.
- Government identification numbers.
- Health information.
- Access or alarm codes.
- Detailed security vulnerabilities.
- Unnecessary identity documents.
- Unrelated confidential records.

---

## 7. Confirmation and Failure

A successful submission shall confirm receipt without confirming service, pricing, or scheduling.

Approved concept:

> Your request has been received for review. Cleanital will evaluate the location, scope, scheduling needs, and current capacity. Submission does not confirm service availability, pricing, scheduling, or acceptance of work.

When delivery fails:

- Do not display success.
- Provide a safe retry or contact option.
- Avoid exposing submitted data.
- Account for duplicate risk.

---

## 8. Routing Record

Each form requires:

1. Purpose and owner.
2. Approved fields.
3. Destination and system of record.
4. Consent or notice.
5. Success and failure behavior.
6. Retention rule.
7. Delivery and spam control.

Fallback, attachment, external processor, and duplicate rules are added only when applicable.

---

## 9. Consent and Communications

Submission authorizes follow-up about that request.

It does not automatically authorize unrelated ongoing marketing.

Before using automated, prerecorded, or mass communication methods, Cleanital shall obtain legal review of the applicable consent and opt-out requirements.

Commercial email practices and telephone or text practices are also governed by CLN-WEB-POL-008.

---

## 10. Career and Contractor Separation

Career and contractor pathways shall remain distinct.

Neither form promises employment, contract award, work volume, compensation, start date, or portal access.

---

## 11. Qualification Ownership

The website collects inputs.

Cleanital’s internal process decides fit, capacity, pricing, assessment, proposal, and acceptance.

No geographic field or automated score may independently confirm service.

---

## 12. Measurement

Primary measures:

- Completed assessment requests.
- Qualified assessment requests.
- Qualification rate.
- Assessment-to-discussion rate.
- Assessment-to-proposal rate.
- Proposal-to-contract rate.
- Revenue influenced where reliable.
- Form completion and failure rates.
- Spam and duplicate rates.
- Portal-routing success.

Traffic and clicks are supporting measures.

---

## 13. Material Form Changes

A form change is material when it changes:

- Purpose.
- Required field.
- Data category.
- Consent.
- Destination or system of record.
- Automated action.
- Attachment capability.
- External processor.
- Qualification logic.
- User commitment or expectation.

Routine label, help-text, or layout changes that preserve meaning may use the lightweight log.

---

## 14. Prohibited Practices

Do not use:

- False urgency or scarcity.
- Deceptive buttons.
- Hidden submission consequences.
- Unnecessary forced disclosure.
- Automatic service confirmation.
- Unapproved instant pricing.
- Bundled unrelated marketing consent.
- Success messages when delivery failed.
