# Cleanital Website Constitution

**Document ID:** CLN-WEB-CON-001  
**Version:** 1.2.0  
**Status:** Ready for Founder Ratification  
**Owner:** Cleanital Founder  
**Canonical Public Domain:** `cleanital.com`  
**Applies To:** The Cleanital public website, its public subdomains, public content, public forms, public integrations, and website publication decisions  
**Effective Date:** Upon Founder ratification  
**Review Cycle:** Annual and upon a proposed change to website purpose, system boundary, public territory model, primary conversion model, or canonical domain  
**Supersedes:** CLN-WEB-CON-001 v1.1.0  

---

## 1. Authority and Precedence

This Constitution is the highest-authority instrument governing the Cleanital public website.

Precedence is:

1. This Constitution.
2. Founder decisions recorded in CLN-WEB-REG-009 that conform to this Constitution and expressly amend any affected normative instrument.
3. Control instruments expressly delegated by this Constitution, including CLN-WEB-CTL-010.
4. Policies and standards identified in CLN-WEB-CTL-010.
5. Approved page specifications and content.
6. Design files, code, tickets, notes, and implementation instructions.

A Register decision that conflicts with this Constitution is void unless the Constitution is first amended under §16.

A lower-authority artifact may implement or summarize a higher rule, but it may not silently replace, narrow, expand, or contradict it.

---

## 2. Intended Function

The Cleanital website exists to:

- Establish credibility.
- Explain Cleanital’s commercial services.
- Educate prospective clients.
- Demonstrate approved results and operating competence.
- Generate qualified commercial opportunities.
- Provide a clear assessment-request path.
- Route existing clients, workers, applicants, contractors, and authorized users to the correct secure experience.
- Publish accurate company, opportunity, legal, and contact information.

The website is a public communication and routing layer. Its detailed system boundary is governed by CLN-WEB-POL-002.

---

## 3. Intended Audiences

The website may serve:

- Prospective commercial clients.
- Existing clients seeking a secure portal or public routing information.
- Prospective employees.
- Prospective contractors.
- Workers seeking a secure portal.
- Business partners.
- Facility, account, property, and operations decision-makers.
- Members of the public seeking information about Cleanital.

Public access shall not expose private operational records.

---

## 4. Public Business Identity

Cleanital shall be presented as a commercial janitorial and facility-cleaning company.

The website shall not position Cleanital as:

- A residential maid service.
- A consumer cleaning marketplace.
- A cleaning-products retailer.
- A staffing marketplace.
- A software product.
- A public scheduling platform.
- A guarantor of a client’s legal or regulatory compliance.

---

## 5. Canonical Domain

The canonical public domain is `cleanital.com`.

A change to the canonical domain requires a constitutional amendment and a migration plan addressing:

- Domain ownership.
- DNS.
- TLS certificates.
- Redirects.
- Email and form routing.
- Search indexing.
- Analytics.
- Portal links.
- Legal notices.
- Rollback.

No contractor, developer, marketer, or automated agent may independently change the canonical domain.

---

## 6. Corp4ce Definition

Corp4ce is Cleanital’s designated secure business-operations platform for authenticated and operational workflows.

The existence of Corp4ce does not mean that every named portal or workflow is active. A client, worker, applicant, contractor, or administrative destination may be publicly linked only after that specific destination is implemented, secured, tested, and approved.

The public website may route to Corp4ce but shall not duplicate the workflows assigned to it by CLN-WEB-POL-002.

---

## 7. Constitutional Prohibitions

Unless this Constitution is amended, the public website shall not:

- Manage work orders.
- Schedule or dispatch service.
- Track worker time or shift status.
- Store operational service records as an independent system of record.
- Display private client, worker, applicant, or contractor records.
- Create binding service contracts automatically.
- Issue binding prices automatically.
- Confirm service availability automatically.
- Process payments or collect payment credentials.
- Duplicate authenticated Corp4ce workflows.
- Expose unrestricted access to Corp4ce data.
- Publish unsupported guarantees, certifications, statistics, testimonials, or endorsements.
- Publish city-, county-, metro-, neighborhood-, or “near me” service-area pages.
- Publish confidential operational information.

---

## 8. Public Territory Control

The exact public service-area rule is owned by CLN-WEB-POL-004.

The following are constitutional controls:

- Public territory is presented at the state level.
- City-level service-area page programs are prohibited.
- Public territory language does not guarantee serviceability.
- A change to the approved states requires a constitutional amendment and operational-capacity analysis.

Other instruments shall cross-reference CLN-WEB-POL-004 rather than create alternate geographic rules.

---

## 9. Conversion Control

The exact primary commercial conversion and its legal meaning are owned by CLN-WEB-POL-007.

The website shall maintain one clearly dominant commercial conversion. A public submission shall not itself create a contract, price, schedule, or service commitment.

---

## 10. Truthfulness and Evidence

The exact claims and evidence controls are owned by CLN-WEB-STD-005.

Marketing value, search value, campaign urgency, visual appeal, or competitive pressure shall not authorize a false, unsupported, expired, or misleading public statement.

---

## 11. Privacy, Security, and Accessibility

The website shall:

- Collect only data authorized for a defined purpose.
- Use controlled integrations and least-privilege access.
- Protect credentials, secrets, and private identifiers.
- Fail safely when a form, portal, or integration is unavailable.
- Be designed and tested against the accessibility target and release criteria owned by CLN-WEB-CTL-010.
- Avoid unsupported privacy, security, or accessibility claims.

Detailed data controls are governed by CLN-WEB-POL-008. Technical, accessibility, repository, and launch controls are governed by CLN-WEB-CTL-010.

---

## 12. Governance Roles

### Founder

- Holds final constitutional and strategic authority.
- Ratifies constitutional amendments.
- Approves changes to canonical domain, purpose, business identity, territory model, primary conversion model, or system boundary.
- Resolves unresolved governance conflicts.

### Website Business Owner

- Maintains alignment with Cleanital’s business strategy.
- Approves public service, industry, conversion, and company content.
- May be held by the Founder.

### Content Owner

- Maintains assigned content.
- Verifies factual accuracy.
- Initiates revision or retirement.

### Technical Owner

- Maintains domain, DNS, hosting, deployment, access, security, backups, forms, analytics, and integrations.
- Shall not independently change public business rules.

### Privacy and Security Reviewer

- Reviews material data collection, tracking, external processors, integrations, and security-sensitive changes.

### Website Incident Owner

- Declares and coordinates website incidents.
- Owns containment, evidence preservation, correction, and escalation.
- Is the Founder unless another person is explicitly designated.

One person may hold multiple roles. Role consolidation does not remove the required control; it changes who records completion.

---

## 13. Proportional Governance

Governance shall be applied proportionally under the materiality test and control tiers owned by CLN-WEB-CTL-010.

A reviewer shall not inflate routine work into unnecessary process or misclassify a material change to avoid review.

---

## 14. Nonconformance

A nonconforming page, form, integration, script, claim, or publication shall be:

- Blocked before publication; or
- Disabled, qualified, or corrected if already live.

A material nonconformance shall be recorded under CLN-WEB-REG-009. Minor corrections may use the lightweight change log.

---

## 15. Exceptions

An exception must:

- Identify the governing rule.
- State the need.
- Define scope and duration.
- State risks and safeguards.
- Name the approver.
- State expiration or review date.
- Be recorded in CLN-WEB-REG-009.

An exception may not be used as a permanent competing rule.

---

## 16. Ratification and Amendment

This Constitution becomes effective when the Founder approves the Phase 1.2 package through a signed record, repository approval, or other durable written approval identified in CLN-WEB-REG-009.

An amendment must include:

- Exact current rule.
- Exact replacement rule.
- Rationale.
- Conflict analysis.
- Applicable privacy, security, data, operational, legal, brand, accessibility, search, and integration impacts.
- Required implementation.
- Effective date.
- Transition and rollback method.
- Founder disposition.

Silent amendment is prohibited.
